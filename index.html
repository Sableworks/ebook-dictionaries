02 — Install

On your e-reader

Exact folder names vary by brand. On PocketBook (Verse and similar):

  1. Show hidden files — Mac: Cmd+Shift+.; Windows: File Explorer → View → Hidden items.
  2. Copy the .dic files into system/dictionaries.
  3. Eject USB, then pick the dictionary on long-press lookup.

Other e-readers that accept the same .dic packs: use that device’s dictionary folder. A different format will not work without conversion.

Stock dictionaries are often tiny — tap a past tense or plural and you get nothing. These packs include lemmas plus common word forms, so lookup hits more often in a novel.
