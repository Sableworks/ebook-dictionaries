Made for reading, not for desk study

Most stock e-reader dictionaries are tiny — tap a past tense or plural and you get nothing. These packs are compiled for real book text: lemmas plus common word forms, so long-press lookup hits more often when you are deep in a novel.

  • German ↔ Polish and English ↔ Polish — both directions
  • Offline — no Wi‑Fi, no account, no cloud
  • .dic format used by many ebook / e-ink readers
  • Open lexical data under CC BY-SA (Wiktionary / FreeDict)
  • Tested on PocketBook (e.g. Verse); other devices that accept the same .dic packs can use them too

Install on your e-reader

Exact folder names vary by brand. On PocketBook (Verse and similar):

  1. Connect over USB (PC Link / mass storage).
  2. Show hidden system files and open system/dictionaries.
  3. Copy the chosen .dic files there.
  4. Disconnect USB, then pick the dictionary on long-press word lookup.

On other e-readers that support the same .dic packs, use that device’s dictionary folder. Different formats will not work without conversion.
